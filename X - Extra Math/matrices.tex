\subsection*{Linear recurrences / Companion matrix}

Consider the linear recurrence
\[
f_n = \sum_{i=1}^k c_i\, f_{n-i}, \qquad n\ge k,
\]
with initial values $f_0,\dots,f_{k-1}$.  Define the $k\times k$ companion matrix $T$ and the $k\times 1$ column vector $F_m$ by
\[
T =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0\\[2pt]
0 & 0 & 1 & \cdots & 0\\[2pt]
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\[2pt]
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1\\[2pt]
c_k & c_{k-1} & c_{k-2} & \cdots & c_1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
F_m =
\begin{pmatrix}
f_m\\[4pt]
f_{m+1}\\[4pt]
\vdots\\[4pt]
f_{m+k-1}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
